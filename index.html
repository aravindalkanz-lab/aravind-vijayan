Research & Publication

Published Journal

Peer-reviewed research on sustainable materials engineering — where civil expertise meets environmental innovation.

Peer-Reviewed Publication

Sustainable Composite Fabric from Water Hyacinth Fiber & Pineapple Leaf Fiber (PALF) for Daily-Use Textile and Eco-Packaging Applications

This study investigates the potential of natural composite fibers derived from Water Hyacinth (an invasive aquatic weed) and Pineapple Leaf Fiber (PALF) as viable, biodegradable alternatives to conventional synthetic materials. The research demonstrates successful fabrication of sustainable fabrics and eco-friendly packaging materials with favorable mechanical properties, contributing directly to circular economy targets and UN SDG goals on responsible consumption, climate action, and clean water. The findings open pathways for scalable, low-cost green material production using abundantly available agricultural waste and invasive plant species.
